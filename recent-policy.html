Recent Policy

2025/11/04
Type: Unilateral Visa-Exemption

"China has announced the extension of its unilateral visa-free entry policy for citizens of 45 countries until December 31, 2026, as part of its continued efforts to boost inbound travel and international engagement. The policy allows ordinary passport holders from participating countries to enter China without a visa for up to 30 days for tourism, business, visiting relatives, or transit. The updated arrangement, effective November 10, 2025, also adds Sweden to the list, joining most EU member states, Singapore, Malaysia, the UAE, and several Latin American countries.
According to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the decision underscores China’s intention to sustain momentum in post-pandemic travel recovery, promote people-to-people exchanges, and enhance business connectivity."
